Story 3 Giving 2 Running it 2
01What makes a charity site trustworthySay who you are, who you help, how money is used and how to reach you, with real (consented) photos and regular updates. 02A clear donate pageOne short reason to give, suggested amounts tied to real outcomes, few form fields, and the payment options your donors actually use. 03Thanking and receipting donorsThank every donor quickly and personally, send the receipt your tax rules require, and tell them what their gift did. 04Sharing impact updatesShort, regular updates (who you helped, what you gave, what's next) build trust; a yearly summary shows the bigger picture. 05Blog and newsletterA simple blog for stories and a monthly email for supporters, with opt-in sign-up and an easy unsubscribe. 06Keeping donor data safeLet the payment provider handle card details, lock down your database, limit who can see donor lists, and never sell or share them.
